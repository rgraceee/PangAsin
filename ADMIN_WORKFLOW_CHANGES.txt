PangAsin Admin, Dashboard, and Demand Changes
Date: 2026-11-04

Summary
-------
This document records the requested admin workflow, account, dashboard, and
supply-and-demand changes, including the database migrations applied for
configurable sector demand.

Admin Workflows
---------------
1. Adding a municipality automatically populates its barangays from the bundled
   PSGC barangay CSV reference data; manual barangay entry is not required.
2. Municipality deletion opens a confirmation dialog with the requested text,
   "Are you sure you want to delete this?", and Yes/No choices.
3. The Validation Queue Select All checkbox has a more visible outline. Its
   action icons were also refreshed.
4. Salt Farmer Master List search has an explicit Search action, supports Enter
   to submit and clearing the search, and does not issue a request on every
   keystroke.
5. Admins can deactivate and reactivate user accounts. Deactivation is recorded
   with a reason.

Account Email and Password Recovery
-----------------------------------
1. Newly provisioned encoder accounts can replace their placeholder email with
   a valid address through an email-change verification flow.
2. The encoder can change the temporary password after signing in.
3. Forgot Password sends a reset link to the account's verified email address.
4. Email-change verification tokens are stored as hashes and are one-time,
   expiring tokens.

Admin Dashboard and Municipality Analytics
------------------------------------------
1. When the dashboard filter is set to Pangasinan/all municipalities, charts
   continue to present municipality-level data.
2. When one municipality is selected, the dashboard charts switch to that
   municipality's barangay-level data. The map was intentionally left unchanged.
3. Municipality Details switches to barangay rows for a single selected
   municipality, including barangay production and registry information.
4. Monthly Production Trend has a year selector populated from available
   production years. Selecting a year loads that year's monthly series.

Demand Benchmarks and Sector Breakdown
--------------------------------------
1. Admins can manage demand benchmarks from a dedicated Demand Benchmark
   Settings page in the admin sidebar, above User Management.
2. Benchmark settings are separated by Philippines (national) and Pangasinan
   (provincial) scope. The year is selected from a dropdown.
3. Pangasinan local supply is calculated from production recorded for the
   selected year and is not manually editable in the benchmark form.
4. Imports are not an editable benchmark-form field. Existing national import
   data is retained for supply calculations.
5. Local demand and supply are labeled with the Pangasinan scope where shown.
6. Demand values used by the dashboard, analytics, public supply-and-demand
   views, and reports are calculated from the configured benchmark for the
   applicable scope and year.
7. Admins can add, edit, and remove sector demand allocations independently for
   Philippines and Pangasinan. Sector percentages are capped at 100 percent per
   scope.
8. Each sector's demand, supply, and produced amounts are calculated from its
   percentage of the applicable scope's benchmark and supply data. National
   supply includes domestic production and imports; Pangasinan supply is based
   on annual local production.
9. The rightmost supply/demand KPI displays the amount of salt still needed to
   meet demand, calculated as max(demand - produced, 0), instead of a shortage
   status label.
10. Removed hard-coded sector demand reference values; public charts and report
    generation use configured benchmark-based sector allocations.
11. Sector settings show the Household, Food Processing, Industry, Agriculture,
    and Others rows for both scopes by default. Each row has an explicit Remove
    action, custom sectors can still be added, and all current edits/removals
    are submitted together when the scope is saved.
12. Household is initialized to 40% for both scopes. Existing national shares
    for Food Processing (26.33%), Industry (17.55%), and Agriculture (9.30%)
    remain unchanged. Others is a read-only residual that automatically fills
    any unused percentage; it is recalculated when sectors are edited/added/
    removed and is never manually allocated by the admin.
13. The dashboard's Salt Needed and Total Production KPI percentages now use
    complementary shares of those two displayed amounts, so together they
    always total 100% when their combined amount is positive.
14. Sector saves now use the successful PUT response directly rather than
    issuing a second settings request immediately afterward. This prevents a
    successful save from being reported as failed if the follow-up refresh
    fails. Expired and insufficient admin sessions now show specific errors.

Database Changes
----------------
The user approved applying the initial pending Alembic revisions to the
configured Supabase database; the later sector-default revisions were also
applied to implement the requested sector behavior:
1. 20261104e001 creates the email_change_tokens table and its indexes.
2. 20261104e002 creates sector_demand_settings and seeds national sector
   percentages derived from the previous sector mix:
   - Household: 46.81%
   - Food Processing: 26.33%
   - Industry: 17.55%
   - Agriculture: 9.30%
3. 20261104e003 seeds the missing sector categories for Philippines and
   Pangasinan, including Others. Provincial shares and the new Others share
   start at 0%; existing sector values are left unchanged.
4. 20261104e004 changes Household to 40% for both scopes and sets Others to the
   remainder after all other configured sectors.

All listed migrations were applied to Supabase. Verification confirmed the
database is at revision 20261104e004; national shares are Household 40%, Food
Processing 26.33%, Industry 17.55%, Agriculture 9.30%, and Others 6.82%.
Pangasinan starts with Household 40%, the other named sectors 0%, and Others
60%.

Post-upgrade verification confirmed:
- Alembic revision is 20261104e004.
- email_change_tokens exists and contained zero rows at verification time.
- sector_demand_settings contains all five sectors for both scopes with the
  percentages listed above.
- No production history or demand benchmark records were modified; the sector
  settings were updated as requested.
